About Us

Jabu's Bakery started as a small-scale business. Started baking in a garage. The business started baking few products but mainly it was bread. Over the years, the business grew and expanded to medium scale business. It was all because of support from local community of Soshanguve and passion for baking. Our business produces freshly baked products and we are committed to provide our customers with the best quality products.

Our Vision

Our vission is to see our community of Soshanguve and surrounding areas having access to freshly baked, out of the oven products.

Our Mission

Our mission is to grow our business and expand to other areas so that people who live far from Soshanguve can also have access to our freshly baked products.
