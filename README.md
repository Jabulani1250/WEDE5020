## Project Overview
The business vision is to see our community of Soshanguve and surrounding areas having access to freshly baked, out of the oven products. The mission is to grow our business and expand to other areas so that people who live far from Soshanguve can also have access to our freshly baked products.
## Website Goals and Objectives
The website's goal is for people who can't come to the bakery to order online no matter how far they are.
